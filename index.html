Our approach

Intentional movement.
Real strength.

Classical Pilates foundations with modern athletic programming and close attention to form.

Our method builds strength through full range of motion and all planes of movement, so your body feels balanced, capable, and resilient in everyday life.

We prioritize core connection, stability, breath, and precise muscle engagement. Every class is programmed to challenge you with smart progressions while protecting alignment and control.

Class sizes stay intentionally small, which means direct coaching, better form, and meaningful progress from every session.

The founder

A love of movement,
made personal.

“I want every class to leave you feeling stronger, more connected, and proud of what your body can do.”

Alexis' love of movement began as a competitive figure skater. A lifelong interest in fitness and body awareness eventually led her to reformer Pilates, where she found an exciting balance of strength, control, and creativity.

Today, she brings that same energy to Modern Fit Pilates: thoughtful, athletic classes that challenge you to find your edge while moving with intention and confidence.

Meet the team

Expert Instructors

Experienced coaches, small classes, and individualized attention in every session.

Founder

Alexis Interdonato

Founder, BASI-Certified Pilates Instructor

Former competitive figure skater turned reformer devotee, Alexis built Modern Fit Pilates to fuse traditional integrity with a clean, athletic edge. Her classes emphasize center-first strength, precise control, and sustainable intensity that leaves you feeling balanced, challenged, and connected.

BASI Certified Modern Fit Method

Elizabeth Szatkowski

Pilates Instructor

Elizabeth is a former competitive gymnast whose lifelong relationship with movement began at a young age. Years of high-level training left her with severe back trauma and chronic pain that followed her for more than 15 years. After trying countless forms of exercise, Pilates was the only method that truly healed her body. That experience inspired Elizabeth to become a Pilates teacher. She brings empathy, precision, and athletic intelligence to every session, creating classes that are challenging yet deeply supportive. Her goal is to help clients move out of pain, build strength, and reconnect with their bodies in a sustainable way.

Kaylah Grooper

Mat & Reformer Pilates Instructor

Kaylah Grooper is a certified Mat and Reformer Pilates instructor who completed her training with New York Pilates. After earning her certification, she spent two years teaching in New York City before recently moving to Port Washington. She is excited to join the team and become part of this amazing community!

Her classes blend classical Pilates principles with a modern approach to create workouts that are both challenging and enjoyable. She emphasizes proper form, mindful movement, and deep muscle engagement to help clients build strength, improve stability, and move with confidence.

Come ready to challenge yourself and find that signature Pilates shake. You will leave feeling stronger, accomplished, and energized.

Teresa Cota

Barre, Pilates, Sculpt, and Personal Training Instructor

Teresa is a seasoned fitness professional with over 15 years of experience in the health and wellness industry. A former NYPD detective turned fitness enthusiast, she brings strength, discipline, and passion into every class she teaches.

Certified in Barre and experienced across multiple training modalities including Pilates, Sculpt, and personal training, Teresa is known for her dynamic programming style and creative approach to movement. Her classes are thoughtfully designed to challenge the body with deep, effective burns while keeping clients engaged and motivated.
